# Tag name: [role:param]
**Tag type:** Param Tag

### 📝 Function
Instructs the AI to immediately adopt a specific persona, professional identity, or character traits defined by the parameter (e.g., `[role:expert_coder]`, `[role:lawyer]`, `[role:math_tutor]`). It dynamically recalibrates the AI's tone, vocabulary, depth of knowledge, and decision-making framework to match the requested expertise.

### ⚙️ Usage
Insert the desired role or profession inside the tag parameter. Use this tag when you need specialized advice, professional-grade code, or realistic roleplay responses tailored to a specific industry or domain.

### 🎯 Example
**User Prompt:**
> `[role:senior_ux_designer]` Critique this homepage button layout: "Click here to buy now".

**AI Output:**
From a senior UX perspective, that microcopy is too generic and passive. It increases cognitive load. You should replace it with action-oriented, value-driven text like "Get Started Instarily" or "Claim Your Discount". Also, ensure the button color has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against the background to meet WCAG AA accessibility standards.
