\# Tag name: \[list]

\*\*Tag type:\*\* Flag Tag



\### 📝 Function

Forces the AI response to display entirely as a list (bullet points or numbered list). It completely strips away all conversational pleasantries, filler text, introductions, and conclusions.



\### ⚙️ Usage

Place the `\[list]` tag anywhere within the prompt. It is highly recommended to place it at the very beginning or the very end of your command for optimal AI recognition.



\### 🎯 Example

\*\*User Prompt:\*\*

> `\[list]` List 3 benefits of morning jogging.



\*\*AI Output:\*\*

1\. Improves cardiovascular health and increases overall physical stamina.

2\. Efficiently burns calories, supporting effective weight management.

3\. Stimulates endorphin release, reducing stress and boosting mental focus.
