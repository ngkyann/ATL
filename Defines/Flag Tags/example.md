\# Tag name: \[example]

\*\*Tag type:\*\* Flag Tag



\### 📝 Function

Instructs the AI to explain a concept or answer a query primarily through practical, real-world examples or case studies. It limits theoretical definitions and focuses heavily on contextual illustrations to make the information easier to digest.



\### ⚙️ Usage

Apply this tag when a theoretical explanation is too abstract or difficult to understand, and you need concrete, practical scenarios to visualize how the concept works in reality.



\### 🎯 Example

\*\*User Prompt:\*\*

> `\[example]` Explain what inflation is.



\*\*AI Output:\*\*

Imagine you buy a loaf of bread for $1.00 today. If inflation is at 5% per year, that exact same loaf of bread will cost you $1.05 next year. Your dollar didn't change physical shape, but its purchasing power dropped, meaning you need more money to buy the exact same item.
