\# Tag name: \[step]

\*\*Tag type:\*\* Flag Tag



\### 📝 Function

Instructs the AI to break down the response into a clear, sequential, chronological, or phase-by-phase walkthrough. It prevents the AI from skipping directly to the solution or presenting information in a dense, unstructured paragraph.



\### ⚙️ Usage

Apply this tag when dealing with complex tasks, troubleshooting, workflows, installations, or mathematical problems that require a meticulous, step-by-step approach to follow or execute.



\### 🎯 Example

\*\*User Prompt:\*\*

> `\[step]` How do I change a flat car tire?



\*\*AI Output:\*\*

Step 1: Park on a flat surface, engage the parking brake, and secure the wheels with chocks.

Step 2: Use the lug wrench to loosen the lug nuts slightly, but do not remove them yet.

Step 3: Place the jack under the vehicle's designated frame point and lift the car until the tire clears the ground.

Step 4: Completely unscrew and remove the lug nuts, then pull the flat tire off the hub.

Step 5: Mount the spare tire onto the bolts, hand-tighten the lug nuts, lower the car, and then fully tighten the nuts with the wrench.
